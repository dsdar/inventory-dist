Set up your Home Inventory Pi

About two minutes of your time, then the Pi installs itself. Keep this page open on your computer, or print it and keep it with the Pi.

  1. Write the card. In Raspberry Pi Imager choose your Pi, then Home Inventory, then the card. Skip the customisation it offers.
  2. Start the Pi. Put the card in the Pi and plug it in. Wait about two minutes.
  3. Join the Pi's Wi-Fi. Point your phone's camera at this code and tap Join.
    Or in Wi-Fi settings: Inventory-Setup
    Password 17634843

    It may say "No internet": that's expected.
  4. Open the setup page. An iPhone usually opens it by itself. If not, point the camera at this code and tap the link.
    Or type http://inv in the browser.
  5. Follow the page. Your email, your home Wi-Fi and its password, then Start install. The page shows the install (about 40 minutes on a Pi 3) and opens your inventory when it's ready.

Moved the Pi, or changed your Wi-Fi? The same two codes work: the Pi opens its Wi-Fi again when it can't find yours. Two Pis setting up at the same time? The second one is called Inventory-Setup- and four letters or digits.
